Don't Guess, Ask Wisely

November 2026

别猜，先问清楚。用户的话没说全时，模型应该先提出有用的问题，而不是直接编一个答案。

Don't Guess, Ask Wisely is an EMNLP 2026 oral paper on that behavior. The benchmark is VagueBench. The training method is DialogGRPO. I am a co-first author, with Mutian Bao and Jinjian Zhang. The affiliations on the paper are Zhejiang University, Ant Group, and City University of Hong Kong.

VagueBench starts from real open-domain queries. Each seed is annotated with intent and detail units, then rewritten into an intent-vague version and a fully-vague version. The hidden information is known, so evaluation can check both whether the model asks and whether the question recovers the missing piece.

DialogGRPO organizes candidate questions as a dialogue-state graph and scores each turn with immediate information gain plus lookahead value. It does this offline, so training does not need to call a user simulator while the parameters update.

In the paper, the evaluated baseline models ask on fewer than 11% of underspecified queries. DialogGRPO, trained on Qwen3.5-27B, asks on 88.4% of intent-vague queries and 91.2% of fully-vague queries. On queries that are already complete, it still mostly answers instead of interrupting.
